Privacy Policy

PantryChef · Last updated: April 17, 2026 · Effective: April 17, 2026

PantryChef ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version We collect only what's needed to make the app work. We don't sell your data, show you ads, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and the display name you choose. This is stored securely in Google Firebase Authentication.

Pantry and recipe data

We store the pantry items you add, recipes you save, shopping lists you create, and your cooking history. This data is stored in Google Firestore and linked to your account so it syncs across your devices.

Photos you choose to share

When you use the Scan Fridge feature, you can take a photo or choose one from your library. That image is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI API for ingredient detection and then discarded — we do not store your photos on our servers.

Device storage

We store a small cache of your recently generated recipes and your cooked recipe history locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device except as part of the pantry/recipe sync described above.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate AI-powered recipe suggestions based on your pantry contents
  • To sync your pantry, recipes, and shopping lists across sessions
  • To update your pantry automatically after you mark a recipe as cooked
  • To display real nutrition data and food photography via the Spoonacular API
  • To provide hands-free voice commands during cooking mode (microphone access)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Third-Party Services

PantryChef uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Anthropic Claude AI (ingredient detection & recipe generation) — anthropic.com/privacy. Photos and pantry item names are sent to Claude to generate recipes. Anthropic does not use your data to train their models under their API terms.
  • Spoonacular (recipe nutrition & imagery) — spoonacular.com. Only recipe titles are sent to Spoonacular to look up nutrition information and images.

4. Permissions We Request

  • Camera — to photograph your fridge or pantry for ingredient scanning. Used only when you actively tap "Scan Fridge". We never access your camera in the background.
  • Photo Library — to let you choose an existing photo for ingredient scanning. We do not browse or index your photo library.
  • Microphone — for hands-free voice commands in cooking mode ("next step", "repeat", etc.). Voice data is processed on-device by the operating system's speech recognition and is not sent to our servers.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab, which permanently removes all your data from our servers within 30 days. Local device data (recipe cache, cooked history) is deleted when you uninstall the app.

6. Children's Privacy

PantryChef is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Firebase provides industry-standard security for data at rest. Your password is never stored by us — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase Auth. That said, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. For material changes we'll notify you through the app or by email. Continued use of PantryChef after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
